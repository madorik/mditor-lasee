# Online Bookstore Operations Report, Q3 2026

## 1. Summary

### At a glance
- Q3 revenue was **KRW 1.84 billion**, up 12% on the previous quarter.
- We gained 42,000 new members, {underline}71% of them through the mobile app{/underline}.
  - Sign-ups peaked during the Chuseok holiday coupon campaign.
  - 38% of new members placed a first order within 30 days of signing up.
- Inquiries about late deliveries {red}rose 9% on the previous quarter{/red}, so we are making them a top priority for Q4.
> Revenue figures exclude VAT.

This is the operations team's regular quarterly report. Figures are as of the October 5 close, and figures that are not yet final are marked in the tables with ~~gray~~ {shade}shading{/shade}.

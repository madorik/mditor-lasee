Privacy Policy

MDitor LaSee works without an account and does not keep your documents on its server. Images you add to a document, however, are uploaded to the service’s image storage. Below is the information the service handles and how it is processed.

This English translation is provided for convenience. If it differs from the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

Documents and images

  • Documents, versions and autosaved drafts are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage). The operator cannot see them.
  • Images you add in the editor are uploaded to the service’s image storage (Cloudflare R2), and the document contains the image’s address. When an image is uploaded it is redrawn, which removes extra information such as the location where a photo was taken (EXIF). Image addresses are made hard to guess, but anyone who knows an address can see the image. Don’t add sensitive images.
  • Uploaded images are deleted automatically after 3 days. After that, the image no longer appears in documents that contain it.
  • To build the preview, work out the pages and make PDFs, the document’s content is sent to the server. The server processes the request, returns the result, and does not keep the content.
  • PDFs made with the MCP tool render_pdf are kept on the server temporarily, for up to 30 minutes, so the download link works, and are then deleted.
  • A link made with ‘Copy link with document’ carries the document’s content after the # in the URL. That part is never sent to the server. Anyone who has the link can read the document, so share it with care.

Revise with AI

Only when you use this feature are the document’s content and your instructions sent to Anthropic’s Claude API for processing. What is sent is handled under Anthropic’s policies. Don’t use this feature on documents that contain sensitive information. When the feature is turned off, nothing is sent.

Access logs

To run the service and prevent abuse, your IP address, the time and path of each request, and browser information may be recorded in the logs of our server and hosting provider. For rate limiting, IP addresses are briefly counted in memory.

Ads and cookies

  • This service shows Google AdSense ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors’ cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • See Google’s explanation of how it uses this information.
  • The service itself uses no cookies for login or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Documents stored in your browser are deleted when you delete them in ‘My documents’ or clear the site’s data in your browser. There are no documents on the server to delete.

Images uploaded to the image storage are deleted automatically 3 days after upload. To have one removed sooner, send its address to the contact below.
